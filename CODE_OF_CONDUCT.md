# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain assumptions, accept corrections, credit contributors and respect privacy. Disagreement about design or trading research is welcome when grounded in evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation, credential exposure and repeated personal attacks are not accepted. Do not promote guaranteed returns, pressure people to trade, or use community spaces for scams or unsolicited solicitation.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public conduct concern, report the offending content with GitHub's reporting tools and contact a repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not publish private evidence or personal details. Security vulnerabilities belong in [private security reporting](SECURITY.md), not public conduct threads.

Maintainers may edit/remove content, close or lock a discussion, warn a participant or restrict participation, proportionate to severity and repetition. They should explain decisions when doing so does not disclose private information. A maintainer involved in a dispute should seek an uninvolved reviewer where available.

This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, reviews and other project spaces, and to representation of the project elsewhere. Respectful good-faith appeals may be raised privately through GitHub. No response-time guarantee is implied.

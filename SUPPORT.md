# Support

- **Questions, ideas, research caveats:** use [Discussions](https://github.com/CptAYounes/sfm-fx-new-age/discussions).
- **Reproducible bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/CptAYounes/sfm-fx-new-age/issues/new/choose) with the version, OS, Python/Qt versions and a synthetic reproduction.
- **Security:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never disclose vulnerabilities or credentials publicly.
- **Documentation:** begin with [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md) and the [engineering index](docs/README.md).

Remove tokens, account IDs, local usernames, private paths and personal/broker data from logs and screenshots. Do not upload databases or `.runtime` directories. Community support is best-effort, not financial advice, account management or a guaranteed service.
